📖 About & Significance

Janmashtami celebrates the divine birth of Lord Krishna. Our temple has hosted this festival for over 50 years, spreading love, bhakti, and joy. Your donation supports temple decoration, bhog, prasad, and children activities.

❓ FAQ

What is Janmashtami? ▾
Janmashtami is the celebration of Lord Krishna's divine birth. It is observed with devotion, bhajans, aartis, and festive rituals.
How can I donate? ▾
You can donate directly via the donation section above using UPI, QR code, or by filling the donation form.
Is the event free to attend? ▾
Yes, the event is open to all. Mahaprasad and darshan are free for everyone.
Can I volunteer? ▾
Absolutely! Please fill the donation form and mention your interest in volunteering in the message field.
